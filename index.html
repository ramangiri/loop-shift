      How to play

      Keep your ball moving around the rings. Shift into the golden gap before each coral barrier reaches you.

      Movement & Spark Rush

      Tap follows the blue arc. Swipe toward the centre for one ring inward, or away for one ring outward. Arrow Up and Arrow Down do the same on a keyboard. Collect three outlined amber special coins in succession to enter a five-second safe Spark Rush: gold coins give 100 points each (×10 base points, without combo stacking). Missing a special coin resets the meter. Rush does not increase speed or skip levels. Afterward you have a short safe gap. Daily and weekly courses stay seeded for everyone.

      Your first round

      1. Choose a name. Save a nickname to join the shared Top 10. “Play without ranking” lets you practise without submitting scores.
      2. Choose a challenge and press Play now. The ball starts moving by itself after a short countdown.
      3. Watch the nearest barrier. Its pale outline marks the immediate danger. The golden spark and glow mark the safe gap.
      4. Tap once to shift. On a phone, tap the game area. On a computer, click it or press Space. You do not need to steer or hold the screen.
      5. Let the barrier pass, then read the next gap. Tapping repeatedly can send you back into danger.
      6. After a hit ends the round, try again. Your result shows the score and whether it saved. Retry repeats the same course; Home → Play now gives you a new course.

      Which ring will I move to?

      Level 1 has two rings: each tap switches to the other ring. From Level 2, a short blue arc shows the destination. Each tap moves just one neighbouring ring; the game chooses inward or outward toward the nearest wall’s safe gap.

      If you are already in the safe gap, wait. A second tap before that wall passes can move you back to the blocked ring. An open, dotted gate is safe to pass through.

      Sparks and shields

      • Golden sparks: collect them by passing through their ring. Six sparks fill one shield.
      • One circle around the ball: one shield. Two circles mean two shields. The ball keeps its chosen colour.
      • Shield capacity: one with two to four rings; two from five rings at Level 9.
      • A barrier hit: uses one shield and gives brief protection. With no shield or Fever protection, a hit ends the round.
      • Pink sparks: optional bonuses between walls. Follow the next gap early if it is safe; survival comes first.

      Perfect shifts, combos and Fever

      Move away from a closed barrier when its gold timing outline appears. Clear it safely to earn PERFECT! +25 base points. The precise window is 0.20–0.38 seconds before its centre reaches you. Switching back cancels the attempt. A later narrow escape can earn CLOSE! +5 instead.

      Chain 2, 3 and 5 perfect dodges for ×2, ×3 and ×5 scoring. An ordinary dodge or a hit breaks the chain outside Fever. Six consecutive perfect dodges start five seconds of Fever: invincibility, glowing rings and double points on top of your combo. Fever protects your shields; the chain resets when Fever ends.

      Levels and patterns

      Clear 12 barriers to complete a level. New rings arrive at Levels 2, 5, 9 and 13, up to six rings. Speed increases gradually. Level messages appear above the rings while you keep playing, with a safe gap before the next barriers.

      Patterns change between steady barriers, drifting barriers and gates that open and close. Read the warning above the arena: moving barriers and gates settle before you reach them. Every tenth level is a boss with alternating, sweeping or pulse gaps. Finish all 100 levels to earn the champion trophy.

      Choose your game

      • Play now: the 100-level run. Your highest completed score counts toward the main Top 10 when ranked.
      • Challenge choices: survive five levels, collect 20 sparks or make five perfect shifts. These are goals within the run; completing one does not stop play.
      • 5-level sprint: a short run that finishes after five levels. It is unranked.
      • Daily challenge: two minutes on the same course for everyone that day, with its own Top 10. It needs a saved online nickname and resets at 05:30 IST (00:00 UTC).
      • Practice: choose an unlocked level under Progress & rewards. Practice and the tutorial do not submit scores or unlock rewards.

      Weekly twists and community sparks

      The weekly challenge is two minutes on a fixed course. Rules rotate each Monday at 00:00 UTC: No Fever keeps combos but disables Fever; Double Spark Points doubles gold spark points without doubling shield charge; One Shield Maximum limits every level to one shield slot. Weekly scores have their own board.

      Finish an online ranked run to add its gold sparks to the weekly community goal. Each run contributes once. Collect 100,000 together to unlock the Convergence theme for everyone. Practice and sprint runs do not contribute.

      Your collection and friends

      Clear bosses at Levels 10–100 in the main run to earn ten unique trophies. Finish the run to save the new trophies. Earn Perfect Pilot with a chain of five perfect shifts, Shield Survivor with five consecutive levels without losing a shield, and Six-Ring Master by clearing Level 13. Select an earned title under Progress & rewards.

      Create up to five private groups or join with a friend’s 12-character code. Up to 30 members share a board of their best main-run scores. Only members can view that group; anyone you give the code can join. Your nickname and selected title remain visible on public ranked boards.

      After a loss, “Why did I lose?” plays the last three seconds at half speed in a separate viewer. Coral and white mark the collision; mint marks the safe ring. You can stop or retry immediately. “Share your result” creates a score card you can share or save as an image.

      Pause, sound and saved scores

      Use Pause, or press P or Escape on a computer. Leaving the game pauses it automatically. Music and sound effects have separate switches; enable them after pressing Play if your browser asks. Optional vibration is in Settings.

      Online best on Home is your saved 100-level score. It matches your own “You” row on that board, even if another player is ranked first. Daily and Weekly have separate records. A dash means no online record has been loaded for your player yet. On this device keeps the local record, which can include offline rounds or other players; it is not automatically uploaded. Wait for “Best saved” after a ranked round. If a score is waiting to save, use Retry save on Home or the result screen. Lower scores never replace higher ones. A nickname is a display name, not a login; use the same browser and site to keep your player identity.
