How to play

Five simple things to know.

  1. Tap to shift. The ball moves itself. Blue marks your next destination. HOLD means stay until the barrier passes.
  2. Avoid coral barriers. Tap early enough to land on the open ring.
  3. Collect gold sparks. Six earn a shield. Purple bonuses are optional.
  4. Watch your shield. Each circle around the ball absorbs one hit.
  5. Pause and save. Pause anytime. At five-level checkpoints, Save & Exit returns Home; Resume loads that checkpoint.
