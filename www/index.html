    Perfect shifts, Fever & new patterns

    Switch away from a barrier just as its gold outline appears. Clear it safely to earn PERFECT! +25. Each barrier pays once.

    Listen for the rhythm: tap, tap, wait, tap. A short tone marks each closed barrier’s perfect window. Moving barriers settle before you reach them. Try again repeats your course; Home → Play now starts a new one.

    Chain 2, 3 or 5 perfect dodges for ×2, ×3 or ×5 scoring. An ordinary dodge or a hit resets the chain.

    Six perfect dodges in a row start 5 seconds of Fever: invincibility and double points on top of your multiplier. Your chain restarts when Fever ends.

    Each level clears after 12 obstacles without stopping the ball. The small completion banner fades above the rings. At levels 2, 5, 9 and 13 a new ring unlocks, up to six. Higher levels move faster. Level 1 is a short two-ring warm-up. Each tap or Space switches rings; from Level 2, a hollow marker shows the next target. With three or more rings, the route chooses inward or outward for you. Time your move; tapping again before a wall clears can move you back into danger. Follow the golden sparks through each wall’s safe gap. Six sparks earn one shield; you can hold two from five rings.

    From level 2, barriers drift. From level 3, pulse gates open and close. Bosses rotate between alternating gaps, sweeping gaps and pulse timing. Watch the warning above the arena. Each obstacle settles before you reach it; a dotted gap is safe.
